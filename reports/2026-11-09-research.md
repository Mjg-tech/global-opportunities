- **MCW Global Young Leaders Fellowship 2027** (mcwglobal.org). Open to 18 to 26-year-olds "from all over the world" (age on 1
  June 2027); a virtual community needs assessment, a 10-day training in New York and Burlington, Vermont (20-29 July 2027), then
  ten months on a community project in education, health or economic security with an alumni mentor. MCW covers international
  economy flights, housing, three meals a day and local transport during the training. Passport and visa fees are not covered, a
  USD 100 deposit (refunded after the training) is due by 16 March 2027, and participants pay for any medical care. The page does
  not say "fully funded", so the label is Substantially Funded. Needs two essays, a one-page resume and two recommendation letters.
- **Hurford Youth Fellows Program 2027** (ned.org). Four months in Washington, DC (1 May - 31 August 2027) for people aged 30
  or younger with experience advancing youth participation in democracy; up to three fellows; monthly stipend, basic health insurance
  and round-trip economy travel. The profile records no civic or democracy work, so this is a long shot.
- **Mauritius-Africa Scholarship Scheme 2027** (Ministry of Tertiary Education, Mauritius; call dated October 2026). For a
  bachelor's holder the route is an MPhil or MPhil/PhD at a public university in Mauritius: upper-second degree or average above
  60%, a supervisor's written consent, a short research proposal, a seat application by the end of January 2027, then nomination
  by the national agency (the DRC's is the Ministry of Higher Education in Kinshasa/Gombe; at most 15 nominees per country).
  Funding is partial: tuition up to MUR 100,000 or 160,000 a year, MUR 16,500 a month "as part support", and return airfare that
  is refunded after arrival; the guidelines expect USD 1,000 of the student's own money for the first three months. Earlier cycles
  had this scheme on the watchlist as unverifiable; the watchlist entry was updated.
- **Mastercard Foundation Scholars Program at UC Berkeley** (master's). Citizens of any African country admitted to an eligible
  master's of two years or less (Public Policy, Development Practice, Industrial Engineering and Operations Research, MPH, the MBA
  with Haas funding, and others). The FAQ describes a "comprehensive scholarship" covering travel, tuition, fees, visa and SEVIS
  fees, housing, food and living expenses. Applicants first apply through Berkeley's graduate admissions ("The application for
  the 2027-2028 admissions cycle is open"); those who enter basic details and two recommenders by "November 15" are invited to
  apply for a fee waiver. The page prints no year for that date; read with the open cycle it is 15 November 2026.
