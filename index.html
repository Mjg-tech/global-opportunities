How to read the labels

Verification

  • Verified The deadline, eligibility and funding were read on the organiser's own page on the date shown as last verified.
  • Partially verified Some facts were read on the official page. The rest come from secondary sources and are marked as such in the record.
  • Needs verification The official page could not be read. Everything comes from secondary sources.

Eligibility

  • Likely eligible Every stated criterion is met by the recorded profile.
  • Possibly eligible At least one criterion depends on something the profile does not record, such as exact age or degree grade.
  • Not eligible A stated criterion is clearly not met.
  • Eligibility unknown The criteria could not be established.

Funding

  • Fully funded Used only when the organiser's own page supports it.
  • Substantially, partially, travel or participation funded Some costs stay with you. The detail view lists each expense.
  • Funding unclear The official page read does not say what is paid.

Deadline status

  • Closing soon 14 days or fewer remain.
  • Upcoming Applications have not opened, or the next cycle is expected but not announced.
  • Date unknown No deadline could be confirmed.

Profile used

    Information is checked against official sources but can change without notice. Always confirm on the organiser's website before applying. Never pay an application fee without confirming who the organiser is.
